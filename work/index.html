UltraPixel Cuvée Prestige

Challenge
Show the full range of UltraPixel finishing on a single label, with every technology in register.
Solution
A shaped label built in layers: digital print first, then foil, screen varnish and relief on top.
Materials
Natural textured ivory paper.
Technologies
Digital printing, hot foil, opaque and high-build screen printing, embossing, debossing, microtexture, shaped die-cutting.
Result
One label that lets a buyer judge print, foil, relief and die-cutting together.
