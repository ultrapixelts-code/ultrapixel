On the label

What we can put on a wine label.

Natural and textured papers

Uncoated, laid and cotton-feel stocks that give a label its character before any ink is on it.

Hot foil

Gold, silver, holographic or custom, for logos, frames and fine lettering.

Embossing and debossing

Relief raised from the paper or pressed into it, alone or in register with foil.

Screen printing and tactile varnish

Dense opaque colour and thick varnish you can feel under a fingertip.

Shaped die-cutting

Profiles that follow the design: points, curves and recesses.

Everything in register

Print, foil, varnish and relief landing in the same place, label after label.

Questions

What buyers ask first.

Which materials suit an ice bucket?

We propose materials and adhesives selected for wet and chilled conditions. Tell us how the bottle is served and we send samples to test on your own bottles.

Can you print small runs and limited editions?

Yes. Short runs are printed digitally, with variable data and numbering where needed.

Can foil and embossing be combined on the same detail?

Yes. Foil can be applied flat or raised, in register with the print and the die-cut.

Do you deliver outside Italy?

Yes. We produce in Trieste and deliver across Europe.
