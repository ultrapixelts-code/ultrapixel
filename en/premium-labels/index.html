Questions

What buyers ask first.

What makes a label premium?

The material and the finishing: textured stock, foil, relief and varnish that react to light and touch, all in register.

Can several finishes be combined on one label?

Yes. Print, foil, screen varnish and relief are applied in register on the same label.
