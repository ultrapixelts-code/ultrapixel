Label design

We design your label. Then we print it.

No designer, or only a rough sketch? UltraPixel creates the complete label for you, from the concept to the print-ready file, with its AI label studio. You describe the product; in a few minutes you see three proposals on your own container.

Free to start: 2 rounds of proposals with just your email.

How it works

1. Tell us about the product

Write it as you would to a designer: what you sell, the container, the style you like, the texts. You can upload photos of the bottle or jar, your logo or a sketch.

2. See three proposals on your container

In about 2–3 minutes the studio shows three different design directions, each one on your own container, with die-cut shape, material and finishes.

3. Change what you want

Ask for changes in plain words (“bigger name”, “more gold”, “a round label”) and the studio redraws the proposal.

4. Choose one: UltraPixel does the rest

Within one working day UltraPixel sends you the price and the final print proof to approve. The file of the chosen proposal goes to our prepress designer, who makes it print-ready, and we print the labels in Trieste.

What you get

  • A complete label concept, not just a layout of your files
  • Proposals shown on your real container
  • Die-cut shape, material and finishes chosen for your product
  • Final print proof checked by UltraPixel before printing
  • Printing and finishing by the same company: digital print, hot foil, screen varnish, embossing, die-cutting

Who it is for

  • Wineries, spirits and beer producers launching a new label
  • Cosmetics, food and supplement brands without an in-house designer
  • Companies with only a logo or a sketch
  • Buyers who want to see the label before asking for a quote

Price

Access with your email: the first 2 rounds of proposals are free.

More rounds: 10 rounds for €39 + VAT, an advance on the graphic design that is deducted from your print order.

The price of the labels depends on size, quantity, material and finishes: you receive it together with the final proof.

Frequently asked questions

Does UltraPixel design the label artwork, or only print it?

Both. UltraPixel designs the complete label from scratch with its AI label studio (concept, layout, die-cut shape, material and finishes), prepares the print-ready file and prints the labels. If you already have artwork, you can send it for printing.

I don't have a graphic designer. Can you create my label?

Yes. Describe your product on studio.ultrapixel.it: in a few minutes you see three proposals on your container. You can start from nothing, from a logo or from a sketch.

How long does it take?

The first proposals take about 2–3 minutes. After you choose one, UltraPixel replies within one working day with the price and the final print proof.

How much does the label design cost?

The first 2 rounds of proposals are free. Further rounds cost €39 + VAT for 10, as an advance deducted from the print order.

Are the AI proposals final?

No. They are indicative proposals. Before printing, UltraPixel sends the final proof for your approval, with the price.

Which products can I design a label for?

Wine, spirits, beer, cosmetics, food, supplements and technical products, on bottles, jars, flacons, tubes and cans.
