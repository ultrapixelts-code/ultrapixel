Anatomy of a label

Fourteen details. One extraordinary label.

Print, foil, screen, relief and die, all landing in the same place.

  1. 01Multicolour digital printingBlacks, greys and all illustrated parts, including the fine landscape.
  2. 02Premium textured paperIvory natural stock with visible fibre.
  3. 03Hot foil goldLogo, frames, flourishes, leaves, grapes and lettering.
  4. 04Foil and relief combinedGold that also rises from the surface.
  5. 05EmbossingRaised flourishes, side elements and some lettering.
  6. 06DebossingDecorative areas pressed into the paper.
  7. 07Opaque screen printingDense black and blue in the lower field and the medallion.
  8. 08High-build screen varnishFloral motifs with a strong tactile relief.
  9. 09Selective screen varnishGloss against matt on the dark areas.
  10. 10MicrotextureVery fine relief patterns in grounds and side panels.
  11. 11Raised tactile detailsRaised dots under “Cuvée Prestige”.
  12. 12Complex shaped die-cutA fully contoured, non-rectangular profile.
  13. 13Precision die-cuttingPoints, curves and recesses on the perimeter.
  14. 14Multi-process registrationFoil, relief, screen and print landing together.

Technologies

Technology behind every label.

Digital printingHigh-resolution digital colour, from short runs to industrial volumes.
Hot foilMetallic foil applied with heat and pressure: gold, silver, holographic or custom.
Screen printingDense, opaque colour and thick varnish layers on top of the print.
EmbossingRelief raised from the paper, on its own or in register with foil.
DebossingDetails pressed into the surface for a quieter, engraved look.
High-build tactile effectsThick varnish you can feel under a fingertip.
MicrotexturesFine relief patterns that change how a surface catches the light.
Die-cuttingShaped profiles with points, curves and recesses, cut in register.
Reverse printingPrinting on the adhesive side, seen through the container or the product.
Multilayer / booklet labelsMore room for text without a bigger label.
Variable dataCodes, names, batches and QR codes that change on every label.
NumberingSequential numbers for limited editions and traceability.
LaminationA protective film over the print, gloss or matt.
Colour measurement and controlColour measured with a spectrophotometer and checked against the target.
