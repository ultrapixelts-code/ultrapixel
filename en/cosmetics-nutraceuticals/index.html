What to tell us

Six things that make a quote exact.

  1. Container: material, shape and diameter
  2. How the product is used and stored
  3. Contents the label may touch: oils, alcohol, creams
  4. Quantity per reference and number of references
  5. Finishes you want: foil, tactile, gloss or matt
  6. Application: by hand or by machine

Questions

What buyers ask first.

Can you print opaque white on clear film?

Yes. Opaque white is printed under or beside the colours on transparent film, for a no-label look.

Can foil be added to a transparent label?

We combine transparent materials with opaque white and tactile effects. For foil on film, tell us the material and we confirm it with a sample.

Do you print small batches?

Yes. Short runs are printed digitally, with several references in the same job.

Can I test the labels on my containers?

Yes. Request samples and try them on your own packs before ordering.

Do you deliver outside Italy?

Yes. We produce in Trieste and deliver across Europe.
