Three directions for your front label

Bordeaux 750 ml, applied by hand. We suggest 90 × 120 mm instead of 150 × 200 mm, which would climb onto the shoulder and wrap more than half the bottle. Your logo replaces the BU seal.

A — Tradition

Your brief, done properly: ivory felt paper, double gold-foil frame, the name in foil and embossed.

B — The Hills

The hills of Ponzone and your young vines, engraved in green, under a gold sun. Arched die cut.

C — The Toast

Your name is a toast. A large embossed gold seal like sealing wax, clean typography, a burgundy foot.
