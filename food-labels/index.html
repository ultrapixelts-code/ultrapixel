What to tell us

Six things that make a quote exact.

  1. Container and surface
  2. Storage and use: ambient, chilled, frozen or humid
  3. Contact with oils or liquids
  4. Application: machine, winding direction, core and roll diameter
  5. Quantities and number of versions
  6. Texts and codes that change

Questions

What buyers ask first.

Which materials suit chilled or humid products?

We propose materials and adhesives selected for those conditions and send samples to test on your own packs.

Do your labels run on automatic applicators?

Yes. Rolls are supplied with the winding direction, core and outer diameter your machine needs.

Can you print several versions in one order?

Yes. Versions are printed digitally in the same job.

Can batch codes or dates change on each label?

Variable data can be printed on every label. Tell us what changes and in what format.

Do you deliver outside Italy?

Yes. We produce in Trieste and deliver across Europe.
