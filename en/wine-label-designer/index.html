    On the front label these must be in the same field of vision: designation, alcoholic strength and volume.

      These usually go on the back label (we prepare it during the fine-tuning):

      • Contains sulphites (and other allergens, if any)
      • Bottled by… (name and town)
      • Lot number
      • Energy value and a QR code or link to ingredients and nutrition
      • Environmental labelling for bottle and closure
      • Origin, where required (e.g. Product of Italy)
