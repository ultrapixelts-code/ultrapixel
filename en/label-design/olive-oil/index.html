Label design / Olive oil labels

Create your olive oil label

UltraPixel Studio is an online label design service from the Italian label printer UltraPixel: describe your olive oil and in a few minutes you see three label designs on your own bottle. Choose one and UltraPixel prepares the print file, sends you the final proof with the price and prints the labels.

Design your label ↗How it works →

Free to start: 2 rounds of proposals with just your email.

Tell the studio the name of the oil, the category, the bottle and the style you want, from traditional estate to contemporary design. Upload your logo or a photo of the bottle if you have them. The proposals keep the name of the food and the net quantity in the same field of vision, and the studio points out the statements that a food label needs, such as the origin, the storage conditions, the best-before date, the operator and the lot. Labels for oil need materials that hold up to oily fingers and drips: they are chosen with UltraPixel before printing. You remain responsible for the texts.

What you get

  • Three different label designs on your own container
  • Changes in plain words until you like it
  • Die-cut shape, material and finishes chosen for your product
  • Final print proof and price from UltraPixel within one working day of your choice
  • Printing and finishing by the same company in Trieste

What it costs

Access with your email: the first 2 rounds of proposals are free.

More rounds: 10 rounds for €39 + VAT, an advance on the graphic design that is deducted from your print order.

The price of the labels depends on size, quantity, material and finishes: you receive it together with the final proof.

Frequently asked questions

Will the label resist oil?

UltraPixel chooses the material and the protective finish with you before printing, so that the label holds up to oil on the bottle. It is part of the final proof and the price.

Can I start from the label I have today?

Yes. Upload a photo of your current label and tell the studio what you want to change; it proposes three new directions on your bottle.

How much does the label design cost?

The first 2 rounds of proposals are free. Further rounds cost €39 + VAT for 10, as an advance deducted from the print order.
