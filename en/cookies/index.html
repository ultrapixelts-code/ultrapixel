Cookie policy

No cookies

This website does not set cookies, neither its own nor those of third parties, and it does not load advertising or social-network trackers. For this reason it does not show a cookie banner.

Session storage

During your visit the site keeps a few items in your browser's session storage: the first page you visited, the website that referred you, any campaign parameters in the link you followed and the previous page you viewed. They stay in your browser and are deleted when you close the tab. They are sent to us only if you send a request through a form, as part of that request.

Statistics

Page views and clicks on the request buttons are counted by our own system without cookies and without identifiers that persist between visits. Details are in the privacy policy.

Fonts and files

Fonts, images and scripts are served from this website. No files are loaded from third-party servers.

Changes

If we introduce cookies or third-party services in the future, this page will be updated first and, where required, you will be asked for consent.
