Questions

What buyers ask first.

What is the difference between embossing and debossing?

Embossing raises a detail from the surface; debossing presses it in.

Can relief be combined with foil?

Yes. Foil can be raised with the relief in register.

Does embossing work on textured paper?

Yes. Textured and natural papers take relief well; we confirm the result with a sample.
