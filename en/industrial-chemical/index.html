What to tell us

Six things that make a quote exact.

  1. Surface: material, texture and shape
  2. Conditions: outdoor, temperature, chemicals, abrasion
  3. How long the label has to last
  4. Data that changes: codes, serials, QR
  5. Application: by hand or by machine
  6. Size, quantity and number of versions

Questions

What buyers ask first.

Which label material suits drums and canisters?

It depends on the surface and the conditions. We propose a technical material and adhesive and send samples to test on your containers.

Can you print QR codes and serial numbers?

Yes. Variable data, numbering and QR codes can change on every label.

Do you print hazard and safety content?

We print the hazard and safety content you supply. Responsibility for the regulatory content stays with you.

Can a label carry more text than fits?

Yes, with multilayer labels that open and close.

Do you deliver outside Italy?

Yes. We produce in Trieste and deliver across Europe.
